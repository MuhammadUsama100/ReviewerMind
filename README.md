# ReviewerMind

An AI agent for **stress-testing and improving research papers before submission**.

It helps catch:

* unclear claims,
* weak evidence,
* missing details,
* inconsistent experiments,
* reproducibility gaps,
* overclaiming,
* and writing that reviewers may misunderstand.

> **Do not use ReviewerMind to decide whether a paper should be accepted or rejected.**

Use it as a **manuscript debugger**: find mistakes, improve the writing, strengthen the evidence, and make your paper harder to misunderstand.

AI feedback can be wrong. Verify important technical and mathematical criticisms yourself.
